# 🛡️ Course 9, Module 4: Prepare and Practice for Interviews with AI

Welcome to **Module 4** (the final module of Course 9 and the entire Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate program extension!) of **Accelerate Your Job Search with AI**. This capstone module equips you with advanced interview preparation and execution strategies. You will master interview formats, craft your professional elevator pitch using Gemini, apply the STAR method for behavioral and technical questions, leverage Gemini Notebook and Gemini Live for simulated mock interviews, prepare thoughtful questions for interviewers, handle challenging inquiries with confidence (Julia's advice), and navigate post-interview steps, offer negotiations, and thank-you communications.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **Interview Format Navigation:** Understanding screening calls, technical evaluations, panel interviews, and behavioral assessments in cybersecurity hiring pipelines.
- **Professional Pitch & STAR Method Engineering:** Crafting a compelling elevator pitch and structuring behavioral responses using the Situation, Task, Action, Result (STAR) framework.
- **AI-Assisted Interview Simulation:** Utilizing Gemini Notebook and Gemini Live to conduct realistic, interactive mock interviews and refine answers in real-time.
- **Post-Interview Protocol & Offer Management:** Drafting professional thank-you notes, evaluating job offers, negotiating compensation, and completing course reflection and graduation milestones.
