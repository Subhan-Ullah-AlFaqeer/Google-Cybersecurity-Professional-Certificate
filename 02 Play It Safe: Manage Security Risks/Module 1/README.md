# 🛡️ Course 2, Module 1: Security Domains and Risk Management

Welcome to **Module 1** of **Play It Safe: Manage Security Risks** (Course 2 of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate)! This module provides an in-depth exploration of the CISSP eight security domains and establishes the foundational principles of risk management. You will examine the relationships between threats, risks, and vulnerabilities, explore the specific focus areas of entry-level security analysts, analyze how operational disruptions impact businesses, and learn the essential steps of the NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF).

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **CISSP Security Domains:** Mastering the eight security domains established by ISC² for the CISSP body of knowledge, focusing on the specific domains most relevant to entry-level analysts (such as Security Operations and Asset Security).
- **Threats, Risks, and Vulnerabilities:** Differentiating between the core components of risk analysis:
  - **Vulnerability:** A weakness in an asset that could be exploited by a threat.
  - **Threat:** Any potential event or actor that could harm an asset.
  - **Risk:** The likelihood and impact of a threat exploiting a vulnerability.
- **Business Impact Analysis:** Evaluating how security incidents disrupt core business operations, compromise sensitive data, and cause financial and reputational damage.
- **NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF):** Understanding the structured, multi-step process for managing organizational security risk and integrating risk management across enterprise operations.

## 🎯 Technical Skills Architecture

### 📊 Security Governance & Risk Management
- **Risk Triad Analysis:** Dissecting security scenarios to isolate specific threats, underlying vulnerabilities, and overall business risk.
- **Domain Identification:** Mapping everyday security tasks to standard industry security domains (CISSP).
- **RMF Process Understanding:** Recognizing the multi-stage lifecycle used to identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor organizational risk.

### 🤖 Applied Practice & Professional Readiness
- **Threat Categorization:** Identifying common entry-level operational threats, including unauthorized access attempts and unpatched systems.
- **Impact Assessment:** Quantifying potential business losses resulting from compromised system availability or data breaches.
- **Analyst Workflow Alignment:** Prioritizing daily duties around high-risk vulnerabilities and high-impact threat vectors.
