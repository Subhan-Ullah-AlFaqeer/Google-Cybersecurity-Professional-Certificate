# 🛡️ Course 2: Play It Safe: Manage Security Risks

Welcome to **Course 2** of the **Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate**! Building on the foundational concepts introduced in Course 1, this course takes a deeper operational dive into managing security risks, applying industry frameworks, conducting security audits, and responding to incidents. You will explore the CISSP eight security domains, master the NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF) and NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF 2.0), implement OWASP security principles, analyze telemetry across Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) dashboards, and execute incident response playbooks to defend organizational assets.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **Risk Management & Threat Mechanics:** Differentiating between threats, risks, and vulnerabilities, and executing the steps of the NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF) to protect business operations.
- **Frameworks, Controls & Security Auditing:** Aligning administrative, technical, and physical security controls with the CIA Triad, NIST CSF core functions (Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover), and OWASP principles, while conducting formal security audits.
- **SIEM Telemetry & Log Analysis:** Analyzing log ingestion, event correlation rules, and metric dashboards across industry SIEM tools (such as Splunk Enterprise Security and Google Chronicle) to detect operational threats.
- **Incident Response & Playbook Execution:** Navigating the incident response lifecycle (NIST SP 800-61), leveraging Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response (SOAR) concepts, and using standardized playbooks to contain, eradicate, and recover from security breaches.

## 🎯 Technical Skills Architecture

### 📊 Risk Management & Governance
- **Risk Assessment:** Evaluating threats, vulnerabilities, and business impacts to prioritize risk mitigation strategies.
- **Security Auditing:** Planning, executing, and documenting security audits to verify policy and regulatory compliance.
- **Control Selection:** Implementing administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to maintain Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

### 🤖 Applied Practice & Incident Operations
- **SIEM Investigation:** Ingesting and searching security logs, correlating events, and triaging high-priority alerts.
- **Playbook Execution:** Following structured incident response procedures to contain threats and restore business functions.
- **SOAR Understanding:** Recognizing candidates for automated playbook actions to decrease Mean Time to Respond (MTTR).
