# 🛡️ Course 8, Module 1: Protect Data and Communicate Incidents

Welcome to **Module 1** of **Put It to Work: Prepare for Cybersecurity Jobs** (Course 8 of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate)! This module launches the final course of your professional certificate by reinforcing the security mindset, exploring the strategic importance of data and asset classification, examining event log analysis using AI tooling (Gemini Notebook), and evaluating the critical roles of disaster recovery, business continuity, and incident escalation in maintaining an organization's security posture.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **Security Mindset & Asset Protection:** Cultivating an active security posture, applying data and asset classification frameworks, and safeguarding organizational assets against evolving threats.
- **Event Log Analysis with Advanced Tooling:** Leveraging AI-driven platforms like Gemini Notebook to ingest, query, and analyze complex security event logs during incident triage.
- **Incident Detection, Escalation & Communication:** Differentiating between standard events and security incidents, executing structured escalation protocols, and making impactful decisions under pressure.
- **Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery:** Designing strategies to ensure operational resilience, swift disaster recovery, and minimum service disruption following major security incidents.
