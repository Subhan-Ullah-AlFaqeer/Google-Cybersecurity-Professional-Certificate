# 🛡️ Course 8, Module 2: Escalate Incidents

Welcome to **Module 2** of **Put It to Work: Prepare for Cybersecurity Jobs** (Course 8 of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate)! This module focuses on the critical decision-making and escalation processes required in real-world Security Operations Centers (SOCs). You will explore the operational importance of structured escalation, understand individual roles and responsibilities within incident response chains, evaluate when minor suspicious activities escalate into major data breaches, master escalation timing, and apply professional judgment to determine when and how to escalate security incidents to senior management and cross-functional teams.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **Escalation Fundamentals & Purpose:** Understanding the strategic necessity of timely escalation, preventing alert fatigue, and communicating security risks effectively across business units.
- **Roles & Responsibilities in Escalation:** Identifying stakeholder responsibilities during an active security incident—from frontline SOC analysts to incident commanders, legal counsel, and executive leadership.
- **Trigger Identification & Impact Analysis:** Assessing threat severity, mapping abnormal activities to potential data breaches, and recognizing the tipping point where standard investigation transitions into formal incident escalation.
- **Timing & Precision in Response:** Managing escalation velocity to ensure rapid containment of active threats while maintaining accurate, verifiable documentation.

## 🎯 Technical Skills Architecture

### 📊 Incident Management & Communication
- **Escalation Judgment:** Evaluating ambiguous security telemetry to decide precisely when an incident requires executive or cross-functional escalation.
- **Stakeholder Coordination:** Communicating technical risk factors clearly to non-technical leadership and specialized response teams.
- **Threshold Assessment:** Recognizing early indicators of widespread attacks (e.g., credential stuffing, lateral movement) before minor events compound into major breaches.

### 🤖 Applied Practice & Professional Readiness
- **Decision-Making Under Pressure:** Applying structured frameworks to resolve high-stakes security incidents efficiently.
- **Operational Alignment:** Ensuring all escalation actions comply with organizational communication policies and regulatory requirements.
- **Professional Accountability:** Demonstrating workplace readiness through rigorous incident handling and clear documentation practices.
