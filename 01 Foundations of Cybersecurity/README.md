# 🛡️ Course 1: Foundations of Cybersecurity

Welcome to **Course 1** of the **Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate**! This foundational course introduces you to the cybersecurity profession, establishing the baseline knowledge required for an entry-level cybersecurity analyst. You will explore primary job responsibilities, fundamental technical and transferable skills, historical threat milestones, the CISSP eight security domains, security frameworks and controls (such as the CIA triad and NIST guidelines), cybersecurity ethics, and the primary tools used across modern Security Operations Centers (SOCs).

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **Security Impact & Core Analyst Responsibilities:** Examining how security attacks disrupt organizational operations, and defining the primary responsibilities, workflows, and core skillsets of entry-level security analysts.
- **Threat Evolution & Historical Context:** Tracing how past and present cyber attacks shaped modern security defense practices, standards, and regulatory frameworks.
- **Domains, Frameworks & CIA Triad:** Navigating the CISSP eight security domains, applying security controls (administrative, technical, physical), and utilizing the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) and NIST guidelines to reduce organizational risk.
- **Ethics & Security Tooling Overview:** Understanding the ethical principles that govern security decisions and getting introduced to operational tools including SIEM platforms, packet analyzers, Linux, SQL, and Python.

## 🎯 Technical Skills Architecture

### 📊 Threat Awareness & Security Governance
- **Threat Vector Analysis:** Differentiating between malware, phishing, denial-of-service, and social engineering attacks.
- **Framework Alignment:** Understanding how NIST guidelines and CISSP domains structure enterprise defense systems.
- **Ethical Decision-Making:** Applying legal standards, confidentiality rules, and ethical principles to security investigations.

### 🤖 Applied Practice & Professional Readiness
- **Phishing & Indicator Spotting:** Identifying suspicious links, header anomalies, and social engineering techniques in emails.
- **Security Tool Classification:** Categorizing software tools (SIEMs, packet sniffers, firewalls, operating systems) by their operational role.
- **Portfolio & Career Building:** Crafting a professional statement and mapping out pathways toward entry-level cybersecurity roles.
