# 🛡️ Course 1, Module 2: The Evolution of Cybersecurity

Welcome to **Module 2** of **Foundations of Cybersecurity** (Course 1 of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate)! This module explores how security threats have emerged, transformed, and scaled alongside the mainstream adoption of computers and the global explosion of the Internet. You will investigate historical and modern cyber attacks, analyze attack vectors and their effectiveness, discover how past breaches shaped current security practices, and examine the high-level framework of the eight security domains.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **Historical Context & Threat Evolution:** Tracing the origins of cyber threats from early hobbyist hackers and isolated malware to sophisticated, state-sponsored attacks and modern cybercrime syndicates.
- **Digital Age Attack Vectors:** Understanding common attack types (such as malware, social engineering, denial-of-service, and data breaches) and why certain attacks achieve high operational success rates.
- **Incident Response & Calmness Under Pressure:** Learning best practices from industry professionals on maintaining operational composure and professional clarity during active data breaches and security incidents.
- **Eight Security Domains:** Gaining a high-level overview of the major domains that structure the cybersecurity industry and enterprise defense frameworks.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vector** | Method or pathway used by an attacker to gain access | Requires perimeters, firewalls, and endpoint detection |
| **Motivation** | Financial gain, espionage, disruption, or notoriety | Helps security teams anticipate threat actor behavior |
| **Impact** | Data loss, operational downtime, or reputational damage | Drives incident response planning and disaster recovery |

## 🎯 Technical Skills Architecture

### 📊 Threat Intelligence & Historical Analysis
- **Contextual Threat Awareness:** Understanding how past security failures led to the creation of modern security standards and regulations.
- **Attack Categorization:** Differentiating between malware types, social engineering vectors, and network-based exploits.
- **Domain Orientation:** Recognizing the eight fundamental security domains that govern enterprise protection strategies.

### 🤖 Applied Practice & Incident Preparedness
- **Risk Assessment:** Evaluating why certain attack methods remain effective against organizations and users.
- **Operational Resilience:** Adopting calm, methodical approaches during crisis management and incident triage.
- **Terminology & Concepts:** Expanding professional vocabulary with historical and contemporary security terms.
