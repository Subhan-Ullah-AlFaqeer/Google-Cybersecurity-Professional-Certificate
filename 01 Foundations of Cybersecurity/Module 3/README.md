# 🛡️ Course 1, Module 3: Protect Against Threats, Risks, and Vulnerabilities

Welcome to **Module 3** of **Foundations of Cybersecurity** (Course 1 of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate)! This module focuses on core security architectures, risk mitigation strategies, and professional conduct. You will examine foundational security principles—including the CIA Triad, NIST frameworks, security controls, and compliance standards—while exploring the ethical guidelines and legal principles that govern decisions in cybersecurity.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
- **The CIA Triad & Core Security Principles:** Mastering Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA Triad) to guide defense strategies and protect sensitive organizational assets.
- **Security Controls, Frameworks & Compliance:** Differentiating between administrative, technical, and physical controls. Applying NIST guidelines (such as the NIST Cybersecurity Framework) to align systems with industry compliance standards.
- **Secure Design Principles:** Integrating security controls early in system architectures to reduce vulnerabilities, manage threat vectors, and strengthen organizational security posture.
- **Ethics & Professional Responsibility:** Understanding ethical dilemmas, privacy regulations, confidentiality obligations, and legal boundaries for cybersecurity practitioners.

## 🎯 Technical Skills Architecture

### 📊 Security Frameworks & Risk Mitigation
- **CIA Triad Application:** Evaluating system designs and incident scenarios through Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
- **NIST CSF Mapping:** Leveraging standard frameworks (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) to assess organizational readiness.
- **Control Classification:** Selecting and implementing appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.

### 🤖 Applied Practice & Professional Readiness
- **Ethical Triage:** Navigating complex privacy, data protection, and disclosure scenarios ethically.
- **Compliance Alignment:** Ensuring organizational policies satisfy regulatory standards (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
- **Asset Defense:** Identifying risks, threats, and vulnerabilities to minimize overall attack surface.
